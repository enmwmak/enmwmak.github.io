This webpage compares the HKMC Annuity (HKMCA) Scheme against a self-made annuity scheme. It also calculates the following:

  • Present values of the monthly receivables across years, assuming a fixed inflation rate across years
  • Income from time deposits of the remaining amount in the self-made annuity scheme, assuming a constant deposit rate
  • Amount remaining in the self-made annuity scheme

The year at which the two schemes having the same return occurs when the Remaining Amount column changes from positive to negative.
